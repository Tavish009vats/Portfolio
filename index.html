Railway Management System

ACADEMIC PROJECT

A command-line railway management system I built as a school project while developing my programming foundation.

The project helped me practice Python through a practical, real-world-style application. I worked with CSV files and Pandas to store and manage passenger and train data, while implementing features such as ticket reservation, cancellation, passenger search, train information management, and food ordering with bill calculation.

More than just a school project, this was an important step in my development journey. Seeing the complete application working gave me the confidence that I could turn an idea into a working program—and it was one of the experiences that made me want to pursue a career as a Full-Stack Developer.
